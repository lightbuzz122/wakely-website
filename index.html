FAQ

Questions owners ask us

Will the texts sound like a robot?

No. Every campaign is written for your industry in a warm, natural tone. Replies read like someone from your team.

How old can the leads be?

The fresher the better, but leads from the last one to two years often respond well. We'll tell you honestly what's worth contacting during your free audit.

Is this compliant with UK rules?

Yes, when done properly. We check how each lead was collected, include an opt-out in every message, and work under a data processing agreement with you.

What do I need to provide?

Your list of old enquiries (a spreadsheet export is fine), your services and rough pricing, and access to your calendar. We handle the rest.

How quickly will I see appointments?

Most campaigns go live within two weeks, and first replies usually come in within hours of the first texts going out.

Do you work outside the UK?

We're UK-based and UK-focused, and we're happy to talk to businesses in Ireland too.
